1. Scope and Who We Are

This Privacy Policy describes how Put Credit Spreadz ("we," "us," or "our") collects, uses, stores, and protects your information when you use our mobile application (the "App"). It applies to all users of the App regardless of which version or feature set you are using, including the current paper trading simulation mode and any future live trading features.

Put Credit Spreadz is an individually owned and operated mobile software product, operating as a sole proprietorship under the trade name "Put Credit Spreadz" in the State of New York. It is not a corporation, LLC, or other formal legal entity. For privacy-related questions or requests, please contact us at putcreditspreadz@gmail.com.

By creating an account or using the App, you agree to the data practices described in this Privacy Policy. If you do not agree, please do not use the App.

2. About This App — Current and Future Versions

Put Credit Spreadz is being developed in phases:

Current Version — Paper Trading Simulation

The current version of the App is a paper trading simulation only. It does not execute real trades, access real brokerage accounts, or handle real money of any kind. All trades, balances, and performance figures are simulated and fictional.

In simulation mode, we collect only the minimal data necessary to operate the simulation: your email address, your strategy settings, and your simulated trading activity.

Future Versions — Live Trading Automation

Planned future feature: We intend to introduce optional live trading automation in a future version of the App. This will allow users to securely connect a third-party brokerage account (such as Charles Schwab) and authorize the App to automatically execute real put credit spread strategies on their behalf using the brokerage's official API. This feature will require explicit opt-in and will involve additional data collection as described in Section 4 of this policy.

This Privacy Policy is written to cover both the current simulation experience and planned future live trading features. Sections describing data that is not yet collected are clearly labeled as future functionality. When live trading features are activated in your version of the App, those sections of this policy will become applicable to you.

3. Information We Collect

3.1 Information You Provide Directly

  • Email address: Collected when you create an account. Used for authentication and to identify your data in our database. We do not send marketing emails.
  • Account password: Stored as a hashed, salted credential by our authentication provider (Supabase). We never store or have access to your plaintext password.

3.2 Information Generated by Your Use of the App

  • Strategy settings: The parameters you configure, including target delta, days to expiration, minimum credit, entry window times, spread widths, and ETF symbol selection.
  • Simulated positions: Paper trading positions opened and closed by your automated bot, including strike prices, expiration dates, credit received, and simulated P&L.
  • Trade history and events: A log of all simulated trade activity, strategy check results, and system events associated with your account.
  • Bot state: Your paper trading balance, whether your bot is active or paused, and kill-switch status.
  • Safety events: Records of automated risk controls that were triggered (e.g., kill-switch activations or manual position closures).

Current version only: We do not currently collect real brokerage account numbers, real portfolio data, Social Security numbers, government-issued IDs, payment card information, or any sensitive financial identifiers. All balances and trades in the current App are entirely simulated.

3.3 Device and Technical Information

  • Push notification token: If you grant notification permissions, we store your device's push token to deliver simulated (and in future, live) trade alerts. You can revoke this at any time via your device settings.
  • Device platform: We record whether your device is iOS or Android to format notifications correctly.
  • Error and diagnostic data: If the App encounters an error, limited diagnostic information (nature of the error, which function it occurred in) may be logged to identify and fix bugs. This does not include personally identifiable information beyond your user ID.

3.4 Information We Do Not Collect (Current Version)

  • Real brokerage account numbers, balances, or real transaction history
  • Brokerage login credentials, passwords, or API keys entered by you
  • Social Security numbers, tax identification numbers, or government IDs
  • Payment card or banking information
  • Precise geolocation data
  • Contacts, photos, microphone, or camera data
  • Browsing history or data from other apps on your device

4. Brokerage Account Data (Future Feature)

This section describes planned future functionality. It does not apply to the current version of the App.

When live trading features are introduced, users who choose to connect a brokerage account will be required to explicitly authorize the App to access their brokerage through that brokerage's official OAuth or API system (e.g., the Charles Schwab Developer API). This authorization will be entirely optional and can be revoked by the user at any time through both the App and their brokerage's account settings.

4.1 What Brokerage Data We Will Collect (Future)

Upon authorization of a brokerage connection, we may collect and store:

  • OAuth access tokens: Encrypted tokens issued by the brokerage that allow the App to place trades on your behalf. We do not store your brokerage username or password — OAuth tokens are issued directly by the brokerage without sharing your credentials with us.
  • Account identifiers: Brokerage-assigned account numbers (partially masked) necessary to route orders to the correct account.
  • Real position and order data: A record of orders placed by the App and their resulting positions, used to track your live strategy performance and manage open positions (e.g., executing closing orders).
  • Real-time balance snapshots: Periodic account balance or buying power data retrieved from the brokerage, used solely to enforce risk controls and position sizing.

4.2 What We Will Never Collect

  • Your brokerage username or password
  • Full, unmasked account numbers in any persistent storage
  • Social Security numbers or tax identification numbers
  • Any data from your brokerage account beyond what is necessary to operate the put credit spread automation strategy

4.3 How Brokerage Data Will Be Used

Data retrieved from a connected brokerage account will be used exclusively to:

  • Execute your authorized put credit spread strategy on your behalf;
  • Monitor and close open positions according to your configured exit criteria;
  • Enforce your configured risk controls, including kill-switch and position limits;
  • Display your live trading performance within the App.

We will never use brokerage data for advertising, profiling, or any purpose beyond operating your trading automation.

4.4 Revoking Brokerage Access

When live trading is available, you will be able to disconnect your brokerage account at any time from within the App (Settings → Brokerage → Disconnect). Upon disconnection, your OAuth access token will be deleted from our systems immediately and any pending automation will be stopped. You should also revoke the App's access from your brokerage account's authorized apps settings to ensure full revocation.

5. How We Use Your Information

We use the information we collect only for the following purposes:

  • Providing the App: To authenticate you, store your settings, run strategy checks, and display your trading history (simulated or live).
  • Executing your trading strategy (future live trading): To place, monitor, and close options positions in your connected brokerage account on your behalf, strictly according to your configured parameters.
  • Push notifications: To notify you of trade events (simulated or live), risk events, or kill-switch activations, only if you have granted notification permission.
  • Risk management: To enforce the safety controls and kill-switch logic you configure, including automatic bot pausing when thresholds are exceeded.
  • Debugging and reliability: To identify and fix errors or unexpected behavior in the App.
  • Legal compliance: To comply with applicable laws, respond to lawful requests from authorities, or enforce our Terms of Use.
  • Account deletion: To process your request to permanently delete your account and all associated data.

We do not use your data for advertising, behavioral profiling, or any purpose not listed above.

6. How We Share Information

We do not sell, rent, trade, or share your personal information with third parties for their marketing or commercial purposes.

We may share your information only in the following limited circumstances:

  • Service providers: We share data with vendors who help operate the App (see Section 7). These vendors are contractually restricted from using your data for any purpose beyond providing their services to us.
  • Your brokerage (future live trading): When you authorize a brokerage connection, we transmit trade instructions to that brokerage on your behalf. This is the core function of the live trading feature and occurs only with your explicit prior authorization.
  • Legal obligations: We may disclose your information if required by law, court order, or governmental authority, or if we believe disclosure is necessary to protect the rights, property, or safety of Put Credit Spreadz, our users, or the public.
  • Business transfers: If Put Credit Spreadz is acquired or its assets transferred, your information may be transferred as part of that transaction. We will notify you of any such change by updating this Privacy Policy and, where feasible, by in-app notification before the transfer occurs.

7. Third-Party Services

The App relies on the following third-party services. Each has its own privacy practices, and we encourage you to review their policies:

7.1 Supabase

All user data — including account credentials, strategy settings, positions, and trade history — is stored in Supabase, a Backend-as-a-Service platform. Supabase provides our database, authentication system, and serverless functions. Data is encrypted in transit (TLS) and at rest (AES-256). Supabase is SOC 2 Type II certified. See supabase.com/privacy.

7.2 Expo Push Notifications

Expo provides push notification infrastructure. If you grant notification permission, your device token is stored in our database and passed to Expo's servers solely to deliver your alerts. No other personal information is shared with Expo. See expo.dev/privacy.

7.3 Yahoo Finance (Public Market Data)

The App retrieves publicly available ETF price data from Yahoo Finance to power simulated options pricing calculations. No personal information is transmitted to Yahoo Finance — only the ticker symbol being queried. This data is used purely to make the simulation realistic and, in future, to evaluate live entry conditions.

7.4 Brokerage API Providers (Future Feature)

Future feature: When live trading is introduced, the App will integrate with brokerage APIs (e.g., the Charles Schwab API). Your interaction with brokerage OAuth flows is subject to your brokerage's privacy policy and terms of service. We encourage you to review those documents before connecting your account.

7.5 Apple (iOS Platform)

As an iOS application, the App is subject to Apple's platform policies and interacts with Apple system services (e.g., push notification delivery via APNs). See apple.com/legal/privacy.

8. Data Retention

We retain your data for as long as your account remains active:

  • Account data (email, authentication credentials): Retained until you delete your account.
  • Strategy settings and trading data: Retained until you delete your account.
  • Push notification tokens: Retained until you delete your account or revoke notification permissions on your device.
  • OAuth brokerage tokens (future): Retained until you disconnect your brokerage account or delete your Put Credit Spreadz account, whichever comes first.
  • Error and diagnostic logs: Retained for up to 90 days for debugging purposes, then purged.

Upon account deletion, all data associated with your account is permanently and irreversibly removed from our systems. See Section 9 for instructions.

9. Account and Data Deletion

You have the right to delete your account and all associated data at any time. Per Apple App Store Guideline 5.1.1(v), we provide an easy in-app method to do so — no email or external process required.

How to Delete Your Account (In-App)

  1. Open the Put Credit Spreadz app.
  2. Tap the Settings tab (bottom navigation bar).
  3. Scroll to the Account section at the bottom of the page.
  4. Tap "Delete Account" (shown in red).
  5. Confirm your intent in the first confirmation prompt.
  6. Confirm again in the second prompt — this action is permanent and cannot be undone.
  7. Your account and all associated data are immediately and permanently deleted.

What Gets Deleted

Upon account deletion, the following data is permanently and irreversibly removed:

  • Your authentication account (email, hashed password)
  • All strategy settings and configuration
  • All simulated and live positions (open and closed)
  • All trade history and order records
  • All trade events and safety events
  • Your trading balance and bot state
  • Your push notification token(s)
  • Any stored brokerage OAuth tokens (if connected, future feature)

Deletion is permanent and irreversible. Your trading history and balance cannot be recovered after deletion. If you have a live brokerage account connected at the time of deletion, we will also immediately revoke the stored OAuth token. We strongly recommend manually removing the App's authorization from your brokerage's authorized apps settings as well.

Deletion by Email Request

If you cannot access the App and wish to request deletion by email, contact us at putcreditspreadz@gmail.com with the subject line "Account Deletion Request" and your registered email address. We will process your request within 30 days.

10. Push Notifications

With your explicit permission, the App sends push notifications to alert you when your bot opens or closes a position, when a kill-switch or safety event is triggered, or (in future live trading) when a real order is executed. Notifications relate only to activity initiated by the App on your behalf.

You can opt out of push notifications at any time by going to your device's Settings → Notifications → Put Credit Spreadz and disabling notifications. Your push token will be removed from our servers when you delete your account.

11. Security

We implement reasonable and appropriate technical and organizational measures to protect your information from unauthorized access, loss, misuse, or disclosure:

  • All data transmitted between the App and our servers is encrypted using TLS (Transport Layer Security).
  • All data stored in our database is encrypted at rest using AES-256 encryption via Supabase.
  • Authentication is handled by Supabase Auth using industry-standard hashed and salted password storage.
  • All API endpoints require authenticated user tokens (JWT) scoped so users can only access their own data.
  • Brokerage OAuth tokens (future feature) will be stored encrypted and will be used exclusively by server-side functions that cannot be directly accessed by other users.
  • Access to our database and backend systems is restricted to authorized service accounts only.

No method of electronic transmission or storage is 100% secure. We cannot guarantee absolute security of your information. If you believe your account has been compromised, contact us immediately at putcreditspreadz@gmail.com.

13. California Privacy Rights (CCPA / CPRA)

If you are a California resident, the California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA) and the California Privacy Rights Act (CPRA) provide you with specific rights regarding your personal information.

Your California Rights

  • Right to Know: You may request disclosure of the categories and specific pieces of personal information we have collected about you, the sources, our purposes for collecting it, and the categories of third parties with whom we share it.
  • Right to Delete: You may request deletion of personal information we have collected from you. You can exercise this right directly in-app (see Section 9) or by contacting us.
  • Right to Correct: You may request correction of inaccurate personal information we maintain about you.
  • Right to Opt-Out of Sale or Sharing: We do not sell or share your personal information with third parties for cross-context behavioral advertising. No opt-out action is necessary, but you may contact us to confirm.
  • Right to Limit Use of Sensitive Personal Information: If we collect sensitive personal information (such as brokerage financial data in future versions), you have the right to limit its use to what is necessary to provide the services you have requested.
  • Right to Non-Discrimination: We will not discriminate against you for exercising any of your CCPA/CPRA rights.

Categories of Personal Information Collected

  • Identifiers (email address, user ID)
  • Internet or electronic network activity (app usage, simulated trading activity)
  • Device identifiers (push notification token, device platform)
  • Financial information — future only (brokerage account identifiers, real trading activity if live trading is enabled)

Submitting a California Privacy Request

To exercise your rights, contact us at putcreditspreadz@gmail.com with the subject line "California Privacy Request." We will respond within 45 days as required by law.
